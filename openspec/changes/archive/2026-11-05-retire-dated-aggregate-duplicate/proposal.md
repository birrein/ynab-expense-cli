## Why

A card statement can post a grouped purchase one day after an itemized receipt, making a duplicate aggregate appear separate.

## What Changes

- Permit retirement of a ledger-owned aggregate against historical items with an explicit receipt and one-day date difference.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `actual-dated-aggregate-expense`: Reconcile grouped statement charges to earlier itemized purchases.
