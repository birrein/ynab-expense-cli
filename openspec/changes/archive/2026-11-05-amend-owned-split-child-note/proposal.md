## Why

A child note in a verified split can become stale after a separately audited correction, even though the amounts and source records are correct.

## What Changes

- Add a narrowly scoped note amendment for one child of a source-ledger-owned split.
- Preserve all source metadata, financial fields, siblings and balances with preview, backup, independent verification and audit.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `actual-owned-split-child-note`: Safely amend a descriptive note on one verified split child.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Actual daily adapter, tests and runbook.
