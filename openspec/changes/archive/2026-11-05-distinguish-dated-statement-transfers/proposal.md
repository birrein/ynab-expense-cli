## Why
A verified line-of-credit draw and a separate incoming transfer have the same amount within four days. The current duplicate guard blocks a real movement even when complete statements distinguish both.
## What Changes
- Permit one explicitly identified different-date duplicate candidate only when complete statements and separate source evidence establish distinct transfers.
- Preserve all existing duplicate, backup, lock and verification protections.

### New Capabilities
### Modified Capabilities
- `actual-confirmed-cashflows`: distinguish a statement-verified transfer from a separately sourced matching transfer on another date.
## Impact
Actual cashflow classification, tests, runbook and OpenSpec. No new dependency or remote change.
