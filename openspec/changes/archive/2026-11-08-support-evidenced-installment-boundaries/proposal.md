## Why
An evidenced first installment has no preceding neighbor, and a penultimate installment may have a separately audited final neighbor whose notes include bank provenance. The present exact-text checks prevent correcting their billing dates.

## What Changes
- Accept the first installment using two exact consecutive following neighbors.
- Accept a penultimate installment using two exact preceding neighbors and an independently verified final ledger-owned installment with explicit statement evidence.
- Preserve all existing identity, ownership, audit, backup, lease, and independent-readback controls.

### New Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities
- `actual-historical-installment-date`: support independently evidenced first and penultimate installment boundaries.

## Impact
Historical installment date policy, tests and runbook; no new transaction or change to notes, amounts or remote configuration.
