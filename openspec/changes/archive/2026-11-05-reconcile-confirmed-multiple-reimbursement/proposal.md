## Why

One confirmed bank deposit can reimburse several purchases on a different account; treating it as ordinary income distorts spending analysis.

## What Changes

- Permit evidence-backed classification of the original expenses and one incoming reimbursement in an existing loan category, preserving the bank amount and explicitly identifying any excess.
- Require exact provenance, previews, backup, guarded writes and independent full-budget verification.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `actual-multiple-expense-reimbursement`: Reconcile one evidenced incoming reimbursement against several distinct existing purchases.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Actual daily adapter, source ledger, tests and runbook.
