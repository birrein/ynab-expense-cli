## Why
A verified debt restructuring is invisible in Actual until the original debt and interest allocation are fully reconciled. The user needs future installments visible without creating duplicate expenses.
## What Changes
- Add non-posting, uncategorized reminders for explicitly verified future installments.
- Preserve posted transactions and balances, and retain an unresolved-review audit.
## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `actual-debt-reminders`: Preview and create non-posting reminders for confirmed future debt installments.
### Modified Capabilities
None.
## Impact
Actual adapter, schedules and rules, tests, runbook and private audit.
