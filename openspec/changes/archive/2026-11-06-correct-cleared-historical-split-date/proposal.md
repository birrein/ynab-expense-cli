## Why
A historical split can be correctly categorized and billed but dated incorrectly. Recreating it would duplicate the expense and lose its existing identity.

## What Changes
- Allow a narrowly evidenced same-month correction of an unreconciled cleared historical split's date, preserving its complete contents.
- Require backup, pending audit, safe retry, sync and independent whole-budget verification.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `actual-historical-split-date`: Evidence-backed date correction of a cleared historical split.
### Modified Capabilities
None.

## Impact
Actual adapter, isolated tests, runbook and private audit state. No direct database edits or unrelated budgets.
