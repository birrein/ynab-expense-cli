## Design
Extend historicalDuplicateExpenseRetirement with a mutually exclusive subscriptionReceiptDuplicate mode. Require the exact same account, purchase date, payee, category, and normalized subscription phrase in both historical and retained notes. The retained transaction must have a verified cashflow ledger record with its exact amount, bank statement and original receipt IDs, and must include links to both. The historical entry must be a simple uncleared, unreconciled, unscheduled record outside all source ledgers, with exact prior notes, amount and import ID. The operator independently examines the statement and receipt; identifiers do not prove their contents. Reject inconsistent or incomplete evidence. Preserve the existing backup, delete-only-old, sync, independent complete readback, exact balance delta, and idempotent audit.
