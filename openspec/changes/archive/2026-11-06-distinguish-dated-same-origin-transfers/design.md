## Context

The current same-origin guard requires the prior pair to have the candidate date, although the general duplicate window covers four days. The origin statement identifies separate rows and a distinct recipient report identifies the new card-to-wallet operation.

## Decision

Accept exact matching prior-leg dates at most four days from the valid candidate date. Preserve reciprocal snapshots, different destination, separate statement descriptors, distinct supporting documents, complete conflict set, pre-write revalidation, backup, audit and independent readback. The operator must inspect both documents; metadata does not prove their contents. Invalid, more distant or mismatching prior dates and unexplained conflicts fail closed.
