## Why

A settled merchant refund can arrive in a different account from the original card purchase, and must reduce the original expense without fabricating a transfer or income.

## What Changes

- Accept explicitly evidenced cross-account refunds while retaining the original expense, category, provenance, cumulative refund limit, and independent readback.
- Default refunds continue to require the original account.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `actual-cross-account-refunds`: Evidence-gated refunds in a different account from a confirmed expense.

### Modified Capabilities

## Impact

Actual daily candidate validation and operational documentation; no changes to budgets or account configuration.
