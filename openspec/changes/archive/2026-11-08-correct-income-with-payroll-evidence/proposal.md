## Why

An employer payslip can substantiate the identity and amount of a unique historical payroll advance whose posting date appears in one complete bank statement, even when no separate bank receipt is available.

## What Changes

- Support an explicitly selected payroll-evidence alternative for a unique positive historical income with a later posting date in the same month.
- Preserve the complete bank statement, unique match, full snapshot, preview hash, backup and independent verification controls.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `actual-historical-income-statement-date`: allow a complete covering bank statement and separately inspected employer payslip as supporting evidence.

## Impact

Income-date validator, targeted tests and runbook. No financial mutation occurs without a separate plan and authorized application.
