## Why
A legitimate line-of-credit draw or repayment can be blocked by a different historical movement with the same amount nearby. The statement and separate receipt may identify both operations, but the existing exception only accepts already linked transfers with embedded sources.

## What Changes
- Permit a narrowly evidenced line transfer when the sole nearby conflict is an exact historical income or linked card payment independently identified by a separate receipt and a distinct dated statement row.
- Preserve all other duplicate controls, source identities, lease, backup and independent verification.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
None.

### Modified Capabilities
- `actual-confirmed-cashflows`: distinguish a legacy statement-backed conflict from a separate line-of-credit transfer.

## Impact
Actual cashflow classifier, focused tests, runbook and OpenSpec; no direct database access or new dependency.
