## Why

Two independently evidenced bank statements and the payment receipt show a four-day cross-month posting delay, exceeding the existing three-day limit despite establishing the operation date.

## What Changes

- Extend the existing cross-month evidence mode from three to four days.
- Preserve its two distinct statement IDs, payment receipt, exact snapshots, duplicate checks, pending audit and independent verification.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `actual-historical-transfer-date`: support a four-day documented posting delay.

## Impact

Transfer date policy, tests, specification and runbook; no new financial transactions.
