## Why
A bank statement explicitly transfers a card's international debt into national debt, while the two currencies are in separate Actual budgets. Without both recorded legs the credit-card balances remain inaccurate; treating them as ordinary income or consumption misstates reports.

## What Changes
- Permit a directed, statement-evidenced pair with the exact bank-specified CLP and USD amounts; no conversion calculation or automatic matching.
- Use existing transfer-exclusion categories and a shared reference, preserve ordinary expenses, and independently verify both budgets with backups and durable recovery audit.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `actual-evidenced-cross-currency-debt-transfer`: Explicit statement-backed paired entries between the existing CLP and USD budgets.
### Modified Capabilities
None.

## Impact
Actual adapter, tests and private audit ledger. No native cross-budget links, remote configuration changes, additional writer or direct database modifications.
