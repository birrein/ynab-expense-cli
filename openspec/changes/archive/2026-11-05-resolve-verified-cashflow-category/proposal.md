## Why
Verified bank-sourced expenses may remain uncategorized until later evidence identifies the purchase. The adapter must resolve them without losing bank source or duplicating the transaction.
## What Changes
- Add exact-identity, evidence-backed category and stale-note resolution to verified cashflows.
- Preserve source ledger and all unrelated data, with preview, backup, lock and independent verification.
## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `actual-verified-cashflow-category-resolution`: Categorize a verified uncategorized cashflow in place.
### Modified Capabilities
None.
## Impact
Actual daily adapter, cashflow audit ledger and tests.
