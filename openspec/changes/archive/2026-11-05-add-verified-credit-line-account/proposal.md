## Why

A bank credit line is a separate real liability and its draws and principal repayments must not be recorded as spending or income. The budget lacks a corresponding account and cannot faithfully balance both sides of its transfers.

## What Changes

- Add a narrow, evidence-backed operation to create one zero-opening credit-line account in the chosen existing Actual budget.
- Preserve backup, lease, unique identity, independent verification and audit.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `actual-verified-credit-line-account`: Create a verified zero-opening credit-line account without mutating existing financial records.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Actual Node adapter, tests, CLI forwarding and runbook. No new budget or bank account is created.
